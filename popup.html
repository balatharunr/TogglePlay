How to Use

  1. Open this extension while on a YouTube or Spotify tab
  2. Open another YouTube or Spotify tab in your browser
  3. Return to this tab and click on the second tab from the list below
  4. The toggle pair will be created and enabled automatically!
  5. Now when you play/pause in either tab, the other will automatically pause/play
