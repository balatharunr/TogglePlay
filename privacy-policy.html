Privacy Policy

TogglePlay is designed to enhance your media experience by automatically switching playback between YouTube, YouTube Music, and Spotify tabs.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not track your browsing habits, media consumption, or any other user data.

Permissions Used

We only use the following permissions to facilitate the media switching functionality:

  • tabs - To detect which media tabs are open and manage tab pairing
  • storage - To save your tab pairing preferences locally
  • scripting - To control playback (play/pause) on your media tabs

How It Works

The extension operates entirely within your browser. All data stays on your device. When you pair two media tabs, the extension sends play/pause commands directly between them without any external servers involved.

Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted with the extension update.

Contact

If you have questions or concerns, please leave feedback on the extension's store page.
