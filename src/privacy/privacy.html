Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026 · Version 1.2.1

In short

TogglePlay operates entirely on your device. It doesn't collect, transmit, or store any personal data. No analytics, no tracking, no servers.

Zero data collection · 100% offline

What TogglePlay does

TogglePlay automatically toggles play/pause between media tabs so you can seamlessly switch between music and video without manually pausing one before playing another.

It works exclusively with these platforms:

YouTube YouTube Music Spotify Web Player

Data collection

TogglePlay does not collect, store, or transmit any data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information — we never access your name, email, or any identifiers
  • No browsing history — we don't track which pages you visit or when
  • No media consumption data — we don't record what you watch or listen to
  • No network requests — the extension never contacts any external server
  • No analytics or telemetry — no Google Analytics, no crash reporting, nothing

Permissions explained

We believe in permission transparency. Here's every permission the extension uses and exactly why:

Permission Why it's needed
tabs Detect open media tabs (YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify) so you can pair them in the popup. Used to read tab titles and URLs for display only.
host_permissions Inject content scripts into YouTube, YouTube Music, and Spotify pages to detect play/pause state and send control commands between paired tabs.
storage Coming in v2.0 — Save your preferences (enabled state, sync mode) locally on your device so they persist across browser restarts. Data never leaves your machine.

How it works technically

Everything happens locally in your browser through Chrome's built-in extension messaging system:

  • Content scripts are injected into supported media pages to detect playback state (playing/paused) via the page's native media elements
  • Background service worker coordinates play/pause commands between paired tabs using Chrome's internal messaging — no network involved
  • Popup UI reads your open tabs to let you select which two to pair — tab info is used for display and immediately discarded

Key fact: All communication happens through chrome.runtime and chrome.tabs APIs. No HTTP requests, no WebSockets, no external APIs. Your data never leaves your browser.

Third-party services

TogglePlay uses no third-party services of any kind:

  • No analytics (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • No crash reporting (Sentry, Bugsnag, etc.)
  • No advertising networks
  • No CDNs or external resource loading — even fonts are bundled locally

Changes to this policy

  • Updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  • Never introducing data collection without explicit user consent
